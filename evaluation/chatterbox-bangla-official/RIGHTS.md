# Evaluation audio and reference recording rights

The seven files in `audios/output` are the official generated demonstration outputs, downloaded from BosonLab/chatterbox-bangla at revision `025a56f71bef7fddc9ee878c9e9557d2ca559499`. Captions, exact source links and checksums are preserved in `manifest.json`. Audition them in the app's Settings. They are pronunciation/audio-quality benchmarks, never automatically selected as character references.

The model card declares MIT metadata. The fine-tuning toolkit is separately distributed under Apache-2.0, with component notices retained in its source tree. These declarations do not establish the consent, recording copyright, performance rights or commercial cloning permission for each person in the demo's **Original** column.

The inspected repository/card does not identify a recording owner or provide a per-reference license/consent grant for those Original recordings. No commercial voice-cloning clearance has been verified. Original files have not been downloaded into, or registered with, the production voice library. Their distinct URLs remain in the manifest for provenance investigation. A familiar name, quoted poem, or voice resemblance is not reliable evidence of ownership or permission.

The declared training datasets are ai4bharat/Shrutilipi, ai4bharat/Rasa and SPRINGLab/IndicTTS_Bengali. Their inspected dataset-card metadata declares CC BY 4.0. Dataset metadata is not a blanket clearance for demo reference voices or every downstream commercial use.

Production requires separate, authorized recordings and a saved declaration naming owner/source/license and intended distribution. The app records that declaration; it cannot independently certify its truth. Different characters cannot register the same canonical PCM reference. No demo Original voice is cleared by this report.
