# v7 — contextual direction and Bengali subtitles

The normal interface is unchanged: paste a story, Generate Cartoon, preview and download. No character, timeline, camera or sound editor has been added. Open `Bangla Toon Studio.exe` for the console-owned local server. Closing its console stops its process tree.

The pre-upgrade source is preserved in `workspace/v7-baseline-source.zip`. Character sheets, registered PNG/SVG artwork, voice models and background/sound libraries were not replaced. Previous MP4s remain available.

## What changed

`RuleBasedScriptInterpreter` separates syntax and explicit speech. It recognizes Bengali/English headings, nested `সংলাপ: নাবিল: …`, Bengali action/SFX/ambience headings, parenthetical delivery, and reliable attributed prose such as `নাবিল রায়ানের দিকে তাকিয়ে বলল, "দাঁড়া।"`. It records a separate gaze action and exact spoken words; Rayan becomes a validated visible listener rather than an accidental speaker. Written notes, signs and internal thoughts are not automatically spoken. Physical source-line references are retained, including prose clauses on the same line.

`EmotionAnalyzer` maintains per-character scene context and records semantic emotion, supported rig expression, evidence, heuristic confidence and trigger event. It recognizes 16 semantic categories: neutral, happy, excited, curious, confused, surprised, afraid, nervous, angry, sad, embarrassed, proud, thinking, suspicious, relieved and playful. Negated fear/reassurance differs from fear; a question about someone else's fear does not make the speaker frightened. Explicit acting tags take priority. These are conservative rules and scene context, not perfect natural-language understanding or calibrated probability estimates.

The existing optional structured AI interface remains available only after configuration and explicit privacy/cost consent. No external service is called by default, and no local LLM was installed. AI proposals are schema/source/registry validated; they cannot invent speech, registered identities or executable commands. Validated proposals also pass through the emotion stage.

`IntelligentCartoonDirector` (with the previous `CartoonDirector` import preserved) uses the audio-derived timeline to plan acting, listener gaze, safe camera shots and scene continuity. Character root offsets persist after walking/back-stepping; continuous walking shares one movement interval across dialogue boundaries. Positions, emotion states, location, camera, recent speaker and music mood are inspectable in `direction.json`. Emotion intervals with actual start/end times appear in the master `emotion_track`.

Emotions use supported articulated gestures, eyebrow/eye changes and modest head/torso posture changes. Expressions settle in and persist until changed. Existing foot locks, gait profiles, arm swing, blink and Rhubarb mouth shapes remain. These remain front-view cutout prototypes: the upgrade does not create new faces, profile/back artwork, alternate hand drawings or nonexistent props. Objects are not invented; unsupported physical detail remains subject to diagnostics/essential-action validation.

## Subtitles replace dialogue panels

Both Godot and the Pillow compatibility compositor now draw white, centered Bengali text with a thin dark outline and subtle shadow. There is no large rectangle, name card, speaker prefix, emotion tag or permanent dialogue overlay. At most two lines are placed within safe margins in 720×1280 and 1280×720 outputs.

The text is shaped with HarfBuzz/FreeType using the existing SIL OFL-licensed Noto Sans Bengali font. The shaped glyph mask is slightly strengthened for readability and burned into video as a transparent texture. This avoids depending on the availability of a particular system font or Godot font-import cache.

Each cue starts with its actual approved audio take and ends at that WAV's actual duration, including sub-frame silence padding correctly. No word timings or page durations are guessed from text length. Long dialogue is split into manageable text chunks **before synthesis**; all chunks are actually synthesized, preserving the full spoken text. Every resulting subtitle follows a complete real WAV take. `subtitles.srt` and the master `subtitles` track use the same times. Explicit screen text remains a separate visual event and is not included in speech subtitles or sent to TTS.

## Voice, sound and caching

BosonLab Chatterbox Bangla remains the default local provider. All four voice categories and permanent per-character references remain. Production still requires an authorized recording and a saved permission declaration; no official demo voice has been promoted to a production reference.

Voice acting uses only Chatterbox's actual `exaggeration` parameter, with small bounded changes (0.35–0.65) around the saved base setting. It does not inject emotion tags into spoken text, invent an emotion API, or pitch-shift/speed-stretch the WAV. Unsupported specific delivery requests are not claimed as guaranteed performances. The synthesis version is part of Chatterbox identities so old speech with different acting settings is not mistaken for a matching new take. Cache keys retain speaker, reference hash, model revision, text, emotion and settings.

The existing SoundDirector, licensed local assets, dialogue ducking, explicit music changes, ambience, synchronized footsteps, scene caching, Godot export and FFmpeg/NVENC/CPU fallback remain. The master clock derives from actual speech and authored visual holds; a short script is not padded or truncated to pretend it is a longer episode.

## Evidence and limits

`samples/v7_benchmark_bn.txt` exercises prose attribution, walking, fear, curiosity, surprise, embarrassment, relief, listener gaze, music changes, leaves SFX and Bengali screen text. `scripts/render_v7_evaluation.py` generates real Chatterbox WAVs, real Rhubarb cues and real Godot/FFmpeg videos in both formats. `scripts/audit_v7_media.py` decodes them and checks mouth traces, grounded walking, continuity, subtitle visibility/timing, final PCM and music.

The offline benchmark is explicitly an **unconditioned evaluation** using one built-in voice. Authorized production references are currently unconfigured. It does not establish distinct child voices, reference-conditioned consistency, exact pronunciation, natural acting or commercial clearance. Those require approved recordings and listening review. Automated waveform/MP4 checks and visual frame inspection must not be represented as a human pronunciation evaluation. See `VERIFICATION.md` for executed results and artifact paths.
